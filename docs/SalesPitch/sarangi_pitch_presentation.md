## Slide 2: The Challenge
**Headline:** Managing Two Business Models is Complex
**Points:**
*   **Inventory Chaos:** Tracking ornaments for sale versus costumes and jewelry out on rent often requires multiple systems or manual tracking.
*   **Deposit & Return Friction:** Handling security deposits, return dates, and damage fees manually leads to errors and customer frustration.
*   **Customer Experience:** Shoppers today expect a seamless digital experience—whether they are buying a necklace forever or renting a costume for a weekend performance.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
**Headline:** A Unified, Purpose-Built Platform
**Points:**
*   **One Dashboard:** A single, powerful system that perfectly handles *both* retail sales and rental bookings.
*   **Tailored for Sarangi:** Custom-built features designed specifically for the unique needs of jewelry and costume businesses.
*   **Scalable & Secure:** Enterprise-grade security to protect your data and a cloud-based architecture that grows with your business.

---

## Slide 4: Streamlined Rental Workflows
**Headline:** Rentals Made Effortless
**Points:**
*   **Smart Booking:** Customers can select start and end dates directly on the product page.
*   **Automated Deposits:** The system automatically calculates and charges security deposits alongside the rental fee at checkout.
*   **One-Click Returns:** When a costume or ornament is returned, staff can process the return and refund the exact deposit back to the customer's payment method with a single click.
*   **Damage Tracking:** Built-in damage fee tiers to easily handle deductions for wear, tear, or missing items.

---

## Slide 5: Powerful Retail & Inventory Management
**Headline:** Complete Control Over Your Stock
**Points:**
*   **Hybrid Cart:** Customers can buy an ornament and rent a costume in the exact same transaction. The system handles the complex backend splitting for you.
*   **Real-time Stock Sync:** Never double-book a costume or oversell a piece of jewelry.
*   **Multi-Location Ready:** Track inventory across your warehouse, storefront, or multiple branches.
*   **Rich Product Catalog:** Support for product variants (sizes, colors), high-resolution image galleries, and detailed specifications.

---

## Slide 6: The Admin Experience
**Headline:** Your Business Command Center
**Points:**
*   **Comprehensive Dashboard:** Real-time analytics, revenue tracking, and order queues at a glance.
*   **Customer Relationship Management (CRM):** Track customer histories, past rentals, and communicate directly through an integrated inbox.
*   **Fulfillment Pipeline:** A dedicated Kanban-style pipeline to Pick, Pack, and Ship (or prepare for Store Pickup).
*   **Identity Verification:** Built-in workflow for customers to upload ID documents for high-value rentals, requiring admin approval before fulfillment.

---

## Slide 7: The Customer Experience
**Headline:** A Premium Journey for Your Clients
**Points:**
*   **Stunning Storefront:** A modern, mobile-responsive design that reflects the elegance of Sarangi.
*   **Self-Service Portal:** Customers can log in to view their active rentals, download professional PDF invoices, and manage their wishlists.
*   **Flexible Payments:** Seamless integration for Online Payments (Cards, UPI, Netbanking) and Cash on Delivery (COD).
*   **Abandoned Cart Recovery:** Automated emails to bring customers back if they leave items in their cart.
