Frequently Asked

How long does a rental deposit refund take?

Deposits are refunded within 48 hours of the item passing its return condition check.

Can I extend a rental after booking?

Yes, subject to availability — raise a ticket or call the store at least 24 hours before your return date.

What if a rented item is damaged?

Minor wear is expected and not charged. Damage beyond normal wear is assessed against our published damage-fee tiers, shared at booking.
