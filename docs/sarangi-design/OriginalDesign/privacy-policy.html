Sarangi Jewels ("Sarangi", "we", "us") operates sarangijewels.com and our Kochi store. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you buy or rent from us, and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and delivery/billing addresses
  • Account login details
  • Order and rental booking history
  • Payment details (processed securely by our payment partner — we do not store full card numbers)
  • Government ID proof, where provided for high-value rental bookings
  • Photos or notes from rental condition checks
  • Messages you send us through support tickets or contact forms

2. Why We Collect Rental-Specific Data

Because Sarangi offers rentals in addition to sales, we collect a few categories of information that a typical online store would not:

  • ID proof for certain high-value bridal jewellery rentals, used solely to verify identity and reduce loss/fraud risk.
  • Condition-check photos, taken at pickup and return, used only to fairly assess deposit refunds and any damage charges.
  • Rental dates and booking history, used to manage item availability and prevent double-booking.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process purchases, rental bookings, deposits and refunds
  • To communicate order status, rental pickup/return reminders, and support responses
  • To improve our catalogue and personalise recommendations
  • To send marketing updates, only if you've opted in — you can unsubscribe at any time
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

4. Sharing With Third Parties

We share information only where necessary to operate the business: payment gateways (for processing payments and deposits), delivery and logistics partners (for shipping and rental pickup/return), and SMS/WhatsApp/email providers (for order and booking notifications). We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Payment & Deposit Data

All payments, including rental deposits, are processed through our PCI-DSS compliant payment partner. Sarangi does not store your full card or bank details on our servers. Deposit hold and refund records are retained for accounting purposes as described below.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and order information for as long as your account is active, and rental/transaction records for the period required by Indian tax and consumer protection law. ID proof collected for a rental is retained only as long as needed for that booking and any related dispute window, after which it is securely deleted.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at privacy@sarangijewels.com, subject to our legal recordkeeping obligations for completed transactions.

8. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your cart, and understand how visitors use our site. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for users 18 years and older. Dance costume rentals for minors must be booked by a parent or guardian.

10. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
