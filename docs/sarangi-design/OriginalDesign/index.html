Since The Shop On MG Road

A Kochi Storefront, Now Open To Everywhere

Sarangi began as a family jewellery counter in the heart of Kochi, known for temple-inspired kemp work and honest craftsmanship. Today, the same pieces — and the trusted rental wardrobe our regulars have always relied on for weddings and arangetrams — are a click away.

Every set is inspected in-house before it reaches you, whether it's leaving our shelves for good or coming home to you for a single, unforgettable evening.
