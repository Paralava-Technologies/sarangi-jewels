Where Tradition Meets Timeless Grace

Est. Kochi

A Family Counter That Became A Trusted Wardrobe

Sarangi began as a small jewellery counter on MG Road, Kochi, built on temple-inspired kemp work and a reputation for honesty in every setting. Neighbours came not only to buy their daughter's wedding set, but to borrow a piece for a single function — a habit that grew into our rental wardrobe.

Today Sarangi is both: a fine and imitation jewellery boutique for pieces you'll pass down, and a rental house for bridal sets, classical dance costumes and bridal makeup — because not every grand occasion needs a permanent purchase.
